## Hi, I'm Abdelilah Omari 👋

PhD researcher in **Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing** at the L3IA Laboratory, Faculty of Sciences Dhar El Mahraz, Sidi Mohamed Ben Abdellah University (Fez, Morocco).
Mathematics teacher since 2018, with a Master's degree in Applied Mathematics.

🔬 **Research focus:** using Large Language Models (LLMs) to extract social, economic, and behavioral insights from **multilingual and low-resource text**: French, Arabic, Moroccan Darija, English, Spanish, and Italian.

🎯 **Application areas:** education · tourism · banking and financial inclusion · media and social media analytics
